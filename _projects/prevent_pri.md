Pressure injuries are a significant source of patient harm and healthcare costs, yet detecting them reliably across diverse patient populations remains a challenge. This project develops pragmatic, automated methods for pressure injury detection that work across heterogeneous clinical settings and patient demographics.

This is an ongoing collaboration with [Sharon Sonenblum](https://www.sonenblum.com/), [Vicki Stover Hertzberg](https://www.nursing.emory.edu/faculty-staff/vicki-stover-hertzberg), [Monique Bouvier](https://www.nursing.emory.edu/faculty-staff/monique-bouvier), and [Judy Gichoya](https://med.emory.edu/directory/profile/?u=JGICHOY).
The project is supported by the National Institutes of Health, National Institute of Biomedical Imaging and Bioengineering under award number 1R01EB036579.

Visit the project website at [prevent-pri.github.io](https://prevent-pri.github.io/).
