Biodiversity data is scattered across thousands of databases and natural history collections worldwide, creating a major bottleneck for ecological research. BOND (Biodiversity on Demand) applies deep learning and database integration techniques — including AI-assisted georeferencing and schema matching — to make this data more accessible and interoperable.

This is an ongoing collaboration with [Daniel S. Park](https://bio.unc.edu/faculty-member/park-daniel/) and colleagues at UNC and partner institutions.
