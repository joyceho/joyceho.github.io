Biodiversity data is scattered across thousands of databases and natural history collections worldwide, creating a major bottleneck for ecological research. This project applies deep learning and database integration techniques to make this data more accessible and interoperable.

This is an ongoing collaboration with [Xiao Feng](https://lab.fengxiao.info/), [Daniel Park](https://www.bio.purdue.edu/lab/park/), and [Adam Smith](https://www.missouribotanicalgarden.org/plant-science/plant-science/about-science-conservation/research-staff/article/2636/smith-adam).
The project is supported by the National Science Foundation (NSF) under award number 2554346.
